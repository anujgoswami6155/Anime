Bleach

Released: October 5, 2004

Genre: Action, Supernatural, Adventure

Bleach follows Ichigo Kurosaki, a teenager who gains the powers of a Soul Reaper and takes on the duty of defending humans from evil spirits while guiding souls to the afterlife.

Known for its intense sword fights, deep lore, and memorable characters, Bleach is a cornerstone of the shonen anime genre.
