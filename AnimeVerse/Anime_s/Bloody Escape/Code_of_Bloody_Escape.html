Bloody Escape

Released: April 5, 2021

Genre: Horror, Thriller, Psychological

Bloody Escape follows a group of people trapped in a deadly game where they must escape a series of gruesome and life-threatening challenges. Each challenge brings them closer to death, and they must rely on their wits and survival instincts to make it out alive.

As the stakes rise, trust is tested and the group faces unimaginable horrors. Who will survive the bloodbath and escape the sinister game?
