Chainsaw Man

Released: October 12, 2022

Genre: Action, Horror, Supernatural

Denji, a teenage boy living in poverty, works as a devil hunter to pay off his deceased father’s debt. After being betrayed and killed, he merges with his pet devil Pochita and becomes Chainsaw Man, a being with chainsaws for limbs. He joins the Public Safety Devil Hunters under Makima, hoping for a better life.

As Denji navigates a brutal world of devils, betrayal, and chaos, he struggles to hold onto his humanity while embracing his monstrous new power.
