Dan Da Dan

Releasing: October 2024

Genre: Sci-Fi, Supernatural, Comedy, Action

Dan Da Dan follows Momo Ayase, a girl who believes in ghosts but not aliens, and Okarun, a boy who believes in aliens but not ghosts. A challenge to prove each other's beliefs leads them into a wild journey full of supernatural chaos and sci-fi horror.

As their world is turned upside down, they must work together to survive bizarre encounters and discover their own strange powers.
