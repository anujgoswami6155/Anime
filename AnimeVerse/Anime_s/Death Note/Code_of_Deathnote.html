Death Note

Released: October 3, 2006

Genre: Psychological Thriller, Mystery, Supernatural

Death Note follows Light Yagami, a high school genius who discovers a mysterious notebook that allows him to kill anyone by writing their name. As he seeks to create a new world free of crime, a legendary detective known as L begins to track him down.

Filled with intellectual duels, ethical dilemmas, and suspenseful twists, Death Note is a masterclass in psychological storytelling.
