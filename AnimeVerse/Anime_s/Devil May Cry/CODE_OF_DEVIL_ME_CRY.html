Devil May Cry

Released: June 14, 2007

Genre: Action, Supernatural, Fantasy

Devil May Cry follows Dante, a demon hunter for hire, as he takes on contracts involving demonic threats in a gritty, stylish world.

With fast-paced battles, a sarcastic protagonist, and supernatural lore, the anime captures the flair of the iconic video game series.
