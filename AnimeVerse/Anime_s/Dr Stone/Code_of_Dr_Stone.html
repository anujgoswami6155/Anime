Dr. Stone

Released: July 5, 2019

Genre: Adventure, Sci-Fi, Post-Apocalyptic

After a mysterious flash of light petrifies humanity, high school genius Senku Ishigami awakens thousands of years later to a world reclaimed by nature. Using science as his weapon, Senku aims to rebuild civilization from scratch and uncover the mystery behind the petrification.

Teaming up with friends old and new, he challenges wild nature, rival kingdoms, and time itself to restore the human race — one scientific breakthrough at a time.
