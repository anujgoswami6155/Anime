Dragon Ball

Released: February 26, 1986

Genre: Action, Adventure, Martial Arts

Dragon Ball follows the story of a young boy named Goku as he trains in martial arts and sets out on a quest to collect the seven mystical Dragon Balls, which summon a wish-granting dragon.

With iconic fights, unforgettable characters, and classic humor, Dragon Ball laid the foundation for modern action anime.
