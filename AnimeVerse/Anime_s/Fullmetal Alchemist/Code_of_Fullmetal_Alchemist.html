Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood

Released: April 5, 2009

Genre: Action, Adventure, Fantasy

Edward and Alphonse Elric are brothers who commit the ultimate taboo—human transmutation—in an attempt to bring their mother back to life. The attempt fails, costing Ed an arm and a leg, and Al his entire body.

In search of the Philosopher’s Stone to restore what they’ve lost, the brothers become State Alchemists and uncover dark truths behind the government, war, and alchemy itself.
