Hell's Paradise

Released: April 1, 2023

Genre: Action, Dark Fantasy, Supernatural, Historical

Hell's Paradise centers around Gabimaru, a deadly ninja on death row who is offered a chance at freedom if he can retrieve the Elixir of Life from a mysterious island. Alongside executioner Sagiri, he faces monstrous beings and rival criminals.

With brutal fights, moral dilemmas, and ancient secrets, Gabimaru must confront his own humanity and past while surviving the horrors of the island.
