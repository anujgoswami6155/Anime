Hunter x Hunter

Released: October 2, 2011

Genre: Adventure, Action, Fantasy

Gon Freecss discovers that his long-lost father is a world-renowned Hunter — a licensed professional who specializes in dangerous tasks like tracking down secret treasures, exploring uncharted lands, or hunting monsters. Determined to follow in his footsteps, Gon sets out to pass the rigorous Hunter Exam and meet his father.

Along the way, he befriends Killua, Kurapika, and Leorio, forming bonds and facing deadly challenges that test their strength, intellect, and resolve.
