Mob Psycho 100

Released: July 11, 2016

Genre: Action, Supernatural, Comedy, Psychological

Shigeo "Mob" Kageyama is an awkward middle schooler with immense psychic powers. Hoping to live a normal life, he suppresses his emotions to keep his powers in check. However, as his emotional tension builds, his power threatens to explode.

Mob works under a con man named Reigen, who claims to be a psychic himself. Together, they encounter evil spirits, dangerous psychics, and other threats that challenge Mob's strength — both physically and emotionally.
