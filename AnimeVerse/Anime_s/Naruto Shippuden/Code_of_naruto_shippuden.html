Naruto Shippuden

Released: February 15, 2007

Genre: Action, Adventure, Fantasy

Naruto Shippuden follows the journey of Naruto Uzumaki as he trains to become the Hokage of his village while protecting the ninja world from threats like the Akatsuki and Orochimaru.

Featuring epic battles, emotional storytelling, and the growth of beloved characters, Naruto Shippuden is a masterpiece of shonen anime.
