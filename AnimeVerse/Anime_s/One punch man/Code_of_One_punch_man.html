One Punch Man

Released: October 5, 2015

Genre: Action, Comedy, Superhero

One Punch Man tells the story of Saitama, a seemingly ordinary man who trained so hard he became the strongest superhero—so strong, in fact, that he can defeat any enemy with a single punch. However, his unmatched power has left him bored and frustrated with the lack of worthy opponents.

As he joins the Hero Association and gains new allies and rivals, Saitama searches for meaning in a world where no fight is ever a challenge for him.
