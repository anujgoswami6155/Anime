TBA: Expected 2025

Tarou Sakamoto was once the world’s greatest hitman. But he fell in love, got married, had a kid… and got fat. Now a humble convenience store owner, he’s trying to live peacefully—but trouble always finds him.

With assassins coming after him and his secret past, Sakamoto must defend his quiet life using his deadly skills—while staying true to his wife’s one rule: no killing!
