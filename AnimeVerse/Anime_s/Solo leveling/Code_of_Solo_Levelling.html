Solo Leveling

Released: January 6, 2024

Genre: Action, Fantasy, Adventure

In a world where hunters battle deadly monsters, Sung Jin-Woo is known as the weakest of all. After a near-death experience inside a mysterious dungeon, he gains access to a unique leveling system that allows him to grow stronger beyond limits.

As he rises from E-rank to the most powerful hunter, Jin-Woo uncovers dark secrets behind the dungeons and his newfound powers, changing the world forever.
