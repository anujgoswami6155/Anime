Tokyo Revengers

Released: April 11, 2021

Genre: Action, Supernatural, Drama, Time Travel

Tokyo Revengers follows Takemichi Hanagaki, a down-and-out adult who discovers he can travel back in time to his middle school days. Determined to save his ex-girlfriend from a tragic fate, he infiltrates a notorious gang to change the future.

As he navigates gang wars, friendships, and betrayal, Takemichi must grow stronger to protect those he loves and find redemption for his past mistakes.
