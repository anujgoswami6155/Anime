Toguo Anki

Released: 2024

Genre: Action, Supernatural, Dark Fantasy, School

Toguo Anki tells the story of young exorcists trained to eliminate bloodline-based curses in a brutal, dangerous world. Shiki Ichinose, a boy with a cursed lineage, is forced into this battle, learning to survive and overcome his inherited darkness.

With violent clashes, cursed abilities, and inner conflict, the story explores themes of legacy, redemption, and the burden of power in a decaying society.
