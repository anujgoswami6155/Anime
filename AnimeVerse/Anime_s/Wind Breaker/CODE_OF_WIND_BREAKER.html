Wind Breaker

Released: April 5, 2024

Genre: Action, School, Delinquents

Haruka Sakura transfers to Furin High School, infamous for its brawlers. But unlike others, he's not there to be a hero—he's there to be the strongest. Furin's students, however, fight for justice, and Haruka finds himself pulled into an unexpected path.

Now surrounded by powerful yet righteous classmates, he must navigate loyalty, friendship, and his own thirst for strength.
