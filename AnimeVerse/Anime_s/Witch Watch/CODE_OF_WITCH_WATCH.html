Witch Watch

Released: Upcoming (TBA)

Genre: Comedy, Supernatural, Romance, School

Witch Watch follows Nico, a young witch who begins living with her childhood friend Morihito, an ogre, as her bodyguard. Chaos, misunderstandings, and spells gone wrong ensue in this comedic high school life story with magical mischief.

Adapted from the popular manga, this series blends slapstick, fantasy, and heartfelt moments in a fun ride.
