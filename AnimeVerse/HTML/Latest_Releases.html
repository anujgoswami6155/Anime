Solo Levelling

Audio: Japanese, Korean
Subtitles: English, Bahasa Indonesia, Español, Français
Awards: 2024 Anime Awards Nominee
Genres: Action, Fantasy, Supernatural, Adventure

Solo Leveling is an action-packed anime adaptation of the popular Korean web novel and manhwa by Chugong. It follows Sung Jin-Woo, an E-rank hunter in a world where portals to dungeons full of monsters have opened. After a near-death experience, Jin-Woo gains the power to level up endlessly, making him the world’s strongest hunter. With stunning animation, intense fight scenes, and an epic underdog story, Solo Leveling is a must-watch for fans of power fantasy anime.

Kaiju No. 8

Audio: Japanese
Subtitles: English, Español, Português, Français
Awards: 2024 Anime of the Season Contender
Genres: Action, Sci-Fi, Supernatural, Military

Kaiju No. 8 is a thrilling sci-fi anime adapted from the manga by Naoya Matsumoto. It takes place in a world where monstrous kaiju regularly attack cities, and specialized Defense Force units are tasked with eliminating them. The story follows Kafka Hibino, a cleanup crew worker who unexpectedly gains the power to transform into a kaiju himself. As he joins the fight to protect humanity, Kafka must conceal his monstrous secret while chasing his dream of becoming a hero.

Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End

Audio: Japanese
Subtitles: English, Deutsch, Español, Français
Awards: 2024 Anime of the Year Winner
Genres: Fantasy, Drama, Adventure, Slice of Life

Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End is a beautiful and emotional fantasy anime based on the manga by Kanehito Yamada and Tsukasa Abe. The story follows Frieren, an elven mage who outlives her human companions after defeating the Demon King. As she reflects on her past and seeks to understand the fleeting nature of human life, Frieren sets off on a new journey to honor their memories and create new ones. Known for its heartfelt themes and stunning animation, it’s a quiet masterpiece.

Dandadan

Audio: Japanese
Subtitles: English, Español, Bahasa Indonesia, Français
Awards: Highly Anticipated New Anime
Genres: Supernatural, Sci-Fi, Comedy, Romance

Dandadan is a high-energy supernatural anime based on the manga by Yukinobu Tatsu. It follows Momo Ayase and Okarun, two high schoolers who each believe in different occult phenomena — one in ghosts, the other in aliens. When they explore both and encounter the unexplainable, they are thrown into a whirlwind of bizarre battles, powers, and romantic tension. With wild animation and a chaotic but heartfelt story, Dandadan is a unique and stylish ride.

Sakamoto Days

Audio: Japanese
Subtitles: English, Español, Bahasa Indonesia, Français
Awards: Anticipated 2025 Action Comedy
Genres: Action, Comedy, Slice of Life, Crime

Sakamoto Days follows Taro Sakamoto, a once-legendary hitman who retires from the underworld to live a peaceful life running a convenience store with his family. But the past doesn’t let go so easily, and when old enemies return, Sakamoto has to use his deadly skills — all while trying not to break his vow of non-violence. Blending over-the-top action with hilarious daily-life moments, it’s a fresh and fun take on the retired badass trope.

Bloody Escape

Bloody Escape is a high-octane cyberpunk anime featuring a dystopian world filled with genetically enhanced humans and dark conspiracies. Wind Breaker

Audio: Japanese
Subtitles: English, Español, Bahasa Indonesia, Français
Awards: 2024 New Generation Shonen
Genres: Action, School, Delinquents, Drama

Wind Breaker is a gritty and emotional school action anime that follows Haruka Sakura, a transfer student with a fierce temper and powerful fists. Enrolling in a school known for its delinquent brawlers, he seeks strength and purpose. But as he faces off against others like him, he finds unexpected friendships and new meaning in fighting for something bigger. With crisp animation and raw energy, Wind Breaker delivers classic street-style shonen vibes with a modern touch.
