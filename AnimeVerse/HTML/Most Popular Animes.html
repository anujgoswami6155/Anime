Most Popular Animes

One Piece

Audio: Japanese, English
Subtitles: English, Bahasa Indonesia, Tiếng Việt, ไทย
Awards: 2024 Anime Awards Nominee, 2023 Anime Awards Nominee,
2023 Anime Awards Winner, 2024 Anime Awards Winner
Genres: Action,Adventure,Fantasy,Shonen

One Piece is a legendary anime and manga series created by Eiichiro Oda. It follows the journey of Monkey D. Luffy and his pirate crew in search of the ultimate treasure, the One Piece, to become the King of the Pirates. Known for its adventurous storylines, deep characters, and emotional arcs, it's one of the most beloved and longest-running series in history.

Naruto Shippuden

Audio: Japanese, English
Subtitles: English, Español, Português, Français
Awards: Tokyo Anime Award Winner, Crunchyroll Fan Favorite
Genres: Action, Adventure, Martial Arts, Supernatural, Shonen

Naruto Shippuden continues the story of Naruto Uzumaki, a young ninja determined to protect his village and bring back his friend Sasuke. As Naruto matures, he faces powerful enemies, uncovers secrets of his past, and takes part in the Fourth Great Ninja War. Packed with emotional arcs, epic fights, and deep friendships, it's a cornerstone of modern anime.

Bleach

Audio: Japanese, English
Subtitles: English, Deutsch, Français, Español
Awards: Best Anime Soundtrack Nominee
Genres: Action, Supernatural, Shonen, Sword Fighting

Bleach follows Ichigo Kurosaki, a teenager who becomes a Soul Reaper to protect the living and guide souls to the afterlife. With his sword Zanpakutō, Ichigo battles against Hollows and other powerful enemies. Known for its intense battles, strong character development, and spiritual themes, Bleach is a fan-favorite in the anime world.

Dragon Ball

Audio: Japanese, English
Subtitles: English, Español, Português
Awards: Hall of Fame Series, Classic Shonen Legacy
Genres: Action, Martial Arts, Sci-Fi, Comedy, Adventure

Dragon Ball tells the story of Goku, a Saiyan raised on Earth who goes on legendary adventures while training to become the strongest fighter. With epic battles, world tournaments, and cosmic threats, the series laid the foundation for generations of shonen anime. Its legacy lives on through its sequels and global fanbase.

Death Note

Audio: Japanese, English
Subtitles: English, Deutsch, Français
Awards: Tokyo International Anime Fair Excellence Award
Genres: Psychological, Thriller, Supernatural, Mystery

Death Note is a gripping psychological thriller about Light Yagami, a genius who gains the power to kill anyone by writing their name in a notebook. As he attempts to become a godlike ruler, a brilliant detective named L begins to close in. The show is praised for its clever mind games, moral questions, and high-stakes tension.

Attack on Titan

Audio: Japanese, English
Subtitles: English, Español, Français, Deutsch
Awards: Anime of the Year, Best Action, Best Opening Sequence
Genres: Action, Dark Fantasy, Drama, Post-Apocalyptic

Attack on Titan is a high-stakes dark fantasy anime set in a world besieged by giant humanoid Titans. Humanity hides behind massive walls, but after a catastrophic breach, young Eren Yeager joins the fight to reclaim freedom. With intense action, mystery, and political intrigue, the series explores themes of survival, freedom, and sacrifice.

One Punch Man

Audio: Japanese, English
Subtitles: English, Español, Português
Awards: Best Comedy, Best Animation Nominee
Genres: Action, Comedy, Superhero, Parody

One Punch Man follows Saitama, a hero who can defeat any opponent with a single punch—but is constantly bored by his own strength. Set in a world full of bizarre monsters and eccentric heroes, the anime blends high-octane action with hilarious satire, poking fun at typical superhero and shonen tropes.

Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood

Audio: Japanese, English
Subtitles: English, Español, Français
Awards: Best Storytelling, Fan Favorite Classic
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Fantasy, Steampunk

Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood follows brothers Edward and Alphonse Elric who use alchemy in a quest to restore their bodies after a failed experiment. Their journey uncovers a sinister plot involving government secrets and powerful enemies. Known for its powerful story, emotional depth, and stunning animation, it’s considered one of the greatest anime of all time.

Vinland Saga

Audio: Japanese, English
Subtitles: English, Español, Français
Awards: 2020 Anime of the Year Nominee
Genres: Action, Historical, Drama, Seinen

Vinland Saga tells the story of Thorfinn, a young warrior caught in the brutal world of Viking conquest. Driven by revenge for his father’s death, he joins a band of mercenaries and begins a journey that challenges his beliefs about violence, honor, and freedom. With breathtaking animation and powerful character arcs, it’s a standout historical epic.
